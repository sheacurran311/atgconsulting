## Operating model
Free Tools (no checkout), Guided consultant-led delivery, and DIFY (Do-It-For-You, not Dify software). Fractional AI leadership is launch scope, delivered through a scoped recurring advisory agreement, not a new entitlement tier. No billing activation. Existing Google OAuth allowlist remains GSC/GA4 read-only for client SEO; other integrations require client-native configuration or separately authorized architecture review. Internal ATG Google connectors remain distinct from client OAuth. No live outreach, external publishing, or client system changes without approval. Tenant isolation, least privilege, no secrets or sensitive records in public intake, and explicit governance gates are mandatory.

## Service taxonomy
Five divisions: Strategy & Advisory (readiness, fractional AI leadership); Automation & Agents (workflow engineering, context engineering, agentic automation, voice AI); Digital Transformation & Growth (custom internal tools, digital transformation including websites/SEO/AEO); Knowledge, Training & Governance (knowledge management, governance, adoption training); Managed AI & Optimization (performance monitoring). All twelve are launch scope. Commercial readiness does not imply a validated production integration.

## Transformation framework
1. Discover and assess: short public intake, public evidence, 20-minute fit call, secondary discovery, readiness baseline.
2. Strategize and prioritize: service-to-workflow matching, opportunity score, risk gates, ROI scenarios.
3. Architect and implement: deterministic pricing, consultant-approved proposal, client authorization, immutable delivery snapshot, versioned playbooks.
4. Enable and govern: acceptance tests, role-based training, documentation, access controls, rollback and human escalation.
5. Measure and optimize: health, costs, verified outcomes, recurring reviews and roadmap updates.
