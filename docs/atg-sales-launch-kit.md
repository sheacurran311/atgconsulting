# ATG Consulting sales launch kit

## One-page copy for review

**AI transformation for small businesses**

ATG Consulting helps small teams improve how work gets done with practical AI workflows, websites and internal systems. Start with one process you want to improve, then decide whether to expand based on the results.

**Where we can help**

- AI readiness and fractional leadership to prioritize projects, evaluate vendors and establish standards.
- Workflow and agent design to route inquiries, prepare drafts, organize information and handle exceptions.
- Websites, search visibility and internal tools to make it easier for customers and staff to act.
- Knowledge management, training and governance to keep the right information with the right people.
- Ongoing monitoring and improvement with an agreed scope, cadence and service levels.

**How an engagement works**

1. Start with a free assessment and a short fit call.
2. Agree on the business problem, baseline, first deliverable, owner and evidence.
3. Review a bespoke proposal with timing, deliverables, fees, change approval and dependencies.
4. Test and document the work, train the people who own it, and review actual outcomes against the baseline.

Start at [atgconsulting.io/intake](https://atgconsulting.io/intake). See the [Value Realization Framework](https://atgconsulting.io/value-realization) after the new pages are approved for release.

## Demonstration brief

**Illustrative scenario only.** A local HVAC business receives maintenance inquiries, urgent repair calls and installation estimates in the same queue.

| Step | Example redesign | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Find the service | Prioritize repair, replacement and maintenance pages with service territories and hours. | Search clicks and impressions by page from authorized Search Console data. |
| Make a request | Separate emergency calls from appointment and estimate requests. Capture enough context for the dispatcher to act. | Qualified inquiries by service and route, with a documented source and consent boundaries. |
| Triage the request | Prepare a structured summary and route it to a named person. Have the person review follow-up language. | Sample handling time and routing errors against a comparable pre-change period. |
| Review value | Compare actual outcomes, document confounders and record any financially verified impact. | Retain recovered capacity separately from documented cash benefits and actual operating costs. |

The example uses a synthetic business and illustrative measures. A live demonstration requires a reviewed mock site, synthetic requests and no external send action.

## First-call intake and follow-up draft

Start by checking the business's own public site and record the source URL, observation date and limitation. In a fit call, ask which requests carry the most value, where inquiries arrive, who responds today and how the client measures booked work. Confirm the decision owner and scope before drafting the proposal.

An outreach draft may use this format after source, contact and outreach authorization review.

> Hi [name],
>
> I was looking at [public page] and saw [specific observation verified on that page]. If [business outcome] is a priority, I can walk you through one possible change and how you could measure whether it helps.
>
> I run ATG Consulting in Castle Rock and help small businesses improve websites, intake and internal workflows. Would a 20-minute conversation be helpful?
>
> Shea Curran, ATG Consulting

Review sender identity, required contact details, opt-out mechanism and jurisdiction or provider requirements before using externally.

## Proposal and founder portfolio outline

Include business objective and baseline, scoped workstreams, exclusions, client responsibilities, acceptance evidence, delivery timeline, fee and payment schedule, third-party expenses, access ownership, and a separate SLA when support is included. Use a written change order for work outside accepted scope.

Fandomly, TruDeF.AI and Agentbets.gg may be presented as Shea's founder projects after checking links, screenshots and current descriptions. Keep them distinct from ATG client case studies. Publish client results only when the evidence and the client's permission are available.
