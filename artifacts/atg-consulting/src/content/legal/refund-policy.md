# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

We want you to feel confident buying from ATG Consulting LTD. This policy explains when and how you can get a refund.

## 1. The 30-day window
You can request a refund within **30 days of purchase**. If no Guided or Done-for-you work has been performed, you get a **full refund**. If work has been performed, we refund what you paid minus the value of the work already done, as described below.

## 2. Self-serve
**Full refund within 30 days of purchase**, even after your report has been delivered.

## 3. Guided session packages
- If no sessions have taken place, you get a **full refund** within 30 days.
- If some sessions have taken place, we refund the purchase price **minus completed sessions at the per-session rate**. The per-session rate is the package price divided by the number of sessions in the package.
- *Example:* a $997 package with 3 sessions has a per-session rate of $332.33. If you've completed 1 session, your refund is $664.67. If you've completed 2, it's $332.34.
- A session counts as completed if it took place, or if you missed it or cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice.
- After 30 days, unused sessions aren't refundable, but you can still use them within 6 months of purchase.

## 4. Done-for-you
- **Before work starts:** full refund, including your deposit.
- **After work starts:** your 30% deposit becomes non-refundable. We refund the rest of what you paid **minus work performed**:
  - **Milestones (default):** minus the price of each milestone completed or in progress, as listed in your statement of work.
  - **Out-of-scope work:** any approved work outside your statement of work is billed at $150 per hour, with a time log available on request.
- Third-party costs paid on your behalf aren't refundable.
- With any refund, we remove our access to your accounts and confirm offboarding with you.

## 5. Monthly care and other monthly plans
- Cancel anytime from your account page or by email. Cancelling stops future charges.
- Past months aren't refunded. You keep access through the end of the month you've paid for.

## 6. What we can't refund
Subscriptions you buy directly from other companies, such as Grok Bot, Claude, ChatGPT, or tools bought through our referral links. Please contact those providers about their refund policies.

## 7. How to request a refund
Email **shea@atgconsulting.io** from your account email with the subject "Refund request." Include what you bought and when. We'll confirm the amount within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method through Stripe, and banks usually post them within 5 to 10 business days.

## 8. Chargebacks
Please contact us first; we can usually fix a problem faster than your bank. If you file a chargeback for a purchase that is eligible for a refund under this policy, or that we've already refunded, we may pause your account while the dispute is open and share records of the services delivered with our payment processor.

## 9. Changes
Each purchase is covered by the refund policy in effect on the date you bought it.
