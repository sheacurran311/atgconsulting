# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

This policy explains what ATG Consulting LTD ("ATG," "we") collects through atgconsulting.io and our services, how we use it, and your choices. Contact: shea@atgconsulting.io.

## 1. What we collect
- **Account info:** name, email, and login details, handled by our sign-in provider, Clerk.
- **Billing info:** handled by Stripe. We receive payment status and limited details such as card brand and last four digits, never your full card number.
- **Intake answers:** information about your business, tools, and goals.
- **Pasted samples:** text you paste for a preview, such as an email thread or reviews. Kept for up to 7 days, then deleted.
- **In-app AI chat logs:** kept for 30 days, then deleted.
- **Session recordings and transcripts:** Guided and Done-for-you sessions only, and only with your consent.
- **Usage data:** basic logs such as pages visited, device and browser, and AI usage counts, used for security, support, and cost limits.
- **Done-for-you access log:** a record of which accounts you granted us access to and when. It never contains passwords or secrets.

## 2. What we don't collect
- The app never connects to your inbox or calendar.
- We never store your account tokens for other services.
- We never send, post, or book anything on your behalf.
- We don't store the contents of your email or calendar, only samples you choose to paste.

## 3. How we use it
We use your information to provide our services, generate your reports and workflows, run AI features, provide support, process payments, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations. We don't sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## 4. AI processing
Intake answers, pasted samples, chat messages, and session transcripts may be sent to our AI provider, xAI (Grok API), to generate outputs. Under xAI's published API policy, xAI does not train its models on API inputs or outputs without permission, and it stores API requests and responses for up to 30 days to audit for abuse or misuse before deleting them automatically. See xAI's terms for details.

## 5. Who we share it with
Only the service providers we need to run the business:
- Clerk (sign-in)
- Stripe (payments)
- Replit (hosting) and our Postgres database host
- xAI (AI processing)
- Google Workspace (email)
- Google Meet (video sessions and recordings)

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or merger of the business.

## 6. Cookies
We use only essential cookies needed to run the site: sign-in session cookies set by Clerk, and a secure cookie used when ATG views a client portal for support. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and our fonts are hosted on our own servers. If that changes, we'll update this policy and ask for consent where required.

## 7. Support access
ATG can view your portal in read-only mode to help with support. Every view is logged.

## 8. Referral links
Some recommended tools use referral or affiliate links. If you click one, that provider may know you came from ATG. We don't share your intake data with them.

## 9. Emails
We send service emails such as receipts, account notices, and support replies. Any marketing emails will include an unsubscribe link, and we honor opt-outs promptly.

## 10. How long we keep it
- Pasted samples: 7 days.
- AI chat logs: 30 days.
- Recordings and transcripts: 12 months, or sooner if you ask us to delete them.
- Account, intake, and report data: while your account is active, plus 90 days after you close it.
- Billing records: 7 years, as tax law requires.

## 11. Security
We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, access controls, and logging. No system is perfectly secure.

## 12. Your choices and rights
You can access, correct, export, or delete your information by emailing shea@atgconsulting.io. We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws (such as Colorado's or California's) or the EU/UK GDPR. We honor those rights where they apply, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

**EU and UK clients:** we process your data to perform our contract with you, for our legitimate interests (security and improving the service), and with your consent (recordings). Your data is processed in the United States. Where required, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

## 13. Children
Our services are for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 18.

## 14. Changes
We'll post updates here with a new date and email you about material changes.
